Research

My research interests sit at the intersection of development economics, human capital, gender, labour markets, public health and data science.

Research agenda

Health and education inequality, women's labour-market outcomes and economic mobility in Sub-Saharan Africa

I am interested in how inequalities in health and education jointly affect women's labour-force participation, occupational sorting, employment quality, earnings and economic mobility, and in identifying which human-capital investments generate the greatest economic returns.

Dissertations

Cardiovascular Disease Data Collection Standardisation in Primary Healthcare

MSc dissertation · University of Westminster · 2026 · 85/100

A scoping review examining standardisation of cardiovascular-disease data collection in primary healthcare and implications for Sub-Saharan Africa. The dissertation considers how standardised data collection can support more comparable health information.

The Roles of Digital Economy on Quality of Life in Nigeria

BSc Economics dissertation · Ahmadu Bello University · 2021

Undergraduate Economics research examining the relationship between digital-economy development and socioeconomic wellbeing in Nigeria.

Work in progress

Health, education and women’s labour-market outcomes

Research agenda in development

I am developing questions about how health and education inequalities interact over the life course. An initial step is to identify data that link measures of health and education with occupational sorting, employment quality and earnings.

Applied research and policy data

Primary Healthcare Data & Health Systems Research

Natview Foundation / Kaduna State · 2024–2025

Contributed to longitudinal and facility-level health-data work involving 89 primary healthcare facilities across three local government areas, including NHMIS, supply-chain and human-resources-for-health data.

InnoHealth Africa: Maternal Health & Digital Health Implementation

2024–present

Applied work includes telemedicine and a maternal-risk application using obstetric signal-function indicators. These initiatives inform questions for future impact and economic evaluation. Implementation alone cannot establish causal impact.

Methods and tools

Python · R · Stata · SPSS · Power BI · QGIS · ODK · KoboToolbox · CommCare · data visualisation · ETL · evidence synthesis. Current methodological interests include causal inference, quasi-experimental designs, field experiments and longitudinal/administrative-data analysis.
