Yusuf Nasir Ahmad

Research on health, education and labour markets in Sub-Saharan Africa

I work at the intersection of development economics, human capital, public health and data science. My emerging research agenda examines how inequalities in health and education shape women's labour-market participation, earnings, employment quality and economic mobility in Sub-Saharan Africa.

I combine applied research experience in public-sector data, primary healthcare, education technology and digital health with an Economics foundation. My long-term goal is to produce rigorous evidence that helps African governments identify which investments in women's health and education generate the greatest improvements in productivity, labour-market opportunity and inclusive growth.
